Industrial & Jobsite Exhibits

Workplace & Industrial Incident Diagrams

Visualize warehouse, construction site, or plant floor incidents by combining facility layouts with equipment routes, safety perimeters, and source-linked chronologies.

Establishing the Facility or Jobsite Layout

Upload an authorized facility floor plan, site logistics drawing, or aerial construction map in Step 1. Capture shift details, work area identifiers, and exhibit scope in the project metadata.

Separating Personnel & Heavy Equipment Routes

Create distinct colored routes in Step 2 for forklifts, cranes, service vehicles, and on-foot personnel. Sequential waypoints help communicate blind spots, staging paths, and travel direction.

Documenting Exclusion Zones & Physical Evidence

Use Area of interest markers for barricaded zones, staging areas, or overhead work zones. Place Reference / evidence markers where physical measurements, machine controls, or safety signage were photographed.

Building an Auditable Chronology & Evidence Register

Sequence shift handoffs, equipment operations, and emergency response milestones in Step 4. Tag safety logs, training records, photographs, and witness statements in Step 5 so technical reviewers can verify every visual annotation.

Exhibit Readiness Checklist

  • Equipment and worker routes assigned high-contrast, distinct colors
  • Safety barriers, signage, and control switches marked with source references
  • Operational sequence aligned with shift or equipment log timestamps
  • Technical root-cause opinions deferred to qualified safety/engineering experts
Scope Reminder: Incident Reconstruction Studio generates visual exhibits from user-entered data. It does not calculate speeds, braking distances, or collision forces, nor does it establish legal causation or medical diagnoses.
