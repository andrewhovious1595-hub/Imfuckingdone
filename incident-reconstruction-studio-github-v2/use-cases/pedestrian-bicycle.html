Vulnerable Road User Exhibits

Pedestrian & Cyclist Crosswalk Exhibits

Clarify right-of-way, visibility, and trajectory questions in pedestrian and bicycle collisions by organizing roadway geometry and witness records onto a single exhibit.

Capturing Crosswalk & Bike-Lane Geometry

Load a crisp overhead intersection map or site photograph showing marked crosswalks, curb ramps, bike lanes, parked vehicles, and lighting poles in Step 1.

Charting Vulnerable Road User & Turning Vehicle Paths

Plot separate routes in Step 2 for the pedestrian or cyclist and any turning or through-moving vehicles. Numbered waypoints illustrate where entry into the crosswalk or bike lane occurred relative to vehicle movement.

Highlighting Visibility & Contact Annotations

Place Area of interest markers on visual obstructions such as parked trucks, foliage, or street furniture. Mark reported interaction points with Contact markers and anchor witness viewpoints using Reference markers.

Maintaining Strict Medical & Forensic Boundaries

If you annotate a Reported injury area or witness-estimated speed, classify it transparently in the Evidence Register. Illustrative markers summarize party contentions or source records—they do not establish biomechanical causation or medical findings.

Exhibit Readiness Checklist

  • Crosswalk markings, bike lanes, and curb geometry clearly visible
  • Separate routes for pedestrian/cyclist and each motor vehicle
  • Sightline obstructions and lighting fixtures noted as Areas of interest
  • All witness viewpoints and traffic signal phases logged in the timeline
Scope Reminder: Incident Reconstruction Studio generates visual exhibits from user-entered data. It does not calculate speeds, braking distances, or collision forces, nor does it establish legal causation or medical diagnoses.
