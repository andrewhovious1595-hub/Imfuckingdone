Property & Commercial Scenes

Premises Liability & Floor Plan Exhibits

Turn floor plans, architectural schematics, or property photographs into annotated exhibits that clarify spatial relationships in slip, trip, fall, and security incidents.

Selecting Clear Property Plans or Site Photos

Upload a store layout, building floor plan, parking lot map, or authorized scene photograph in Step 1. Document the exact floor, entryway, or exterior zone inside the Scene Notes panel.

Tracing Pedestrian & Staff Movement Paths

Use Step 2 (Routes) to chart the injured party's path of travel alongside maintenance or staff inspection routes. Numbered points help reviewers follow movement into and out of the incident zone.

Annotating Hazards, Cameras & Reported Injury Areas

Use Area of interest markers to identify liquid spills, uneven pavement, stairways, or low-lighting zones. Place Reference / evidence markers at security camera positions or warning sign locations. If using a Reported injury area marker, keep in mind it is illustrative only and not a medical finding.

Logging Notice & Inspection Chronology

In Step 4 (Timeline) and Step 5 (Evidence), document sweep logs, CCTV clip timestamps, incident reports, and prior maintenance tickets while clearly distinguishing verified timestamps from witness estimates.

Exhibit Readiness Checklist

  • Floor plan orientation and primary entrances identifiable
  • Travel route separated from staff inspection or cleanup routes
  • CCTV camera locations and photo vantage points marked as Reference points
  • Notice periods and maintenance records logged in the Evidence Register
Scope Reminder: Incident Reconstruction Studio generates visual exhibits from user-entered data. It does not calculate speeds, braking distances, or collision forces, nor does it establish legal causation or medical diagnoses.
