Evidentiary Transparency

Exhibit Integrity & Classification Standards

Visual exhibits carry persuasive weight only when their evidentiary basis is transparent. Incident Reconstruction Studio is designed around clear boundaries between illustrative visual aids and technical forensic proof.

What This Software Does—and Does Not Do

Incident Reconstruction Studio is a visual exhibit builder. Every route waypoint, marker position, label, timeline entry, and classification is entered by the user. The application does not independently authenticate uploaded imagery, compute vehicle speeds or momentum, simulate biomechanics, establish causation, or generate expert or medical opinions.

The Four-Tier Evidence Classification Model

To prevent confusion during negotiation, mediation, deposition, or trial preparation, the Studio's Evidence Register enforces explicit tagging across four tiers:

ClassificationMeaningRecommended Handling
Documented factSupported by objective measurements, official reports, or authenticated mediaCite exact Bates number, photo ID, or measurement source
Expert interpretationConclusions drawn by an appropriately qualified reconstructionist or specialistAttribute to the expert's report and supporting methodology
AssumptionWorking input used to illustrate a party's version or hypothetical sequenceState explicitly as an assumption in notes and labels
Unresolved / disputedConflicting witness accounts or contested physical positionsAvoid presenting as settled fact; note both positions where applicable

Illustrative Injury-Area Annotations

The Reported injury area marker exists strictly as a visual annotation for referencing documented complaints or records. It does not establish a medical diagnosis, mechanism of injury, anatomical boundary, or clinical opinion.

Authoritative Public Rules & Legal References

When preparing demonstrative exhibits or illustrative aids for legal proceedings, consult the controlling rules in your jurisdiction alongside qualified counsel:

  • Federal Rules of Evidence — Rule 107: Illustrative Aids (Cornell LII) ↗
  • Federal Rules of Evidence — Rule 901: Authenticating or Identifying Evidence (Cornell LII) ↗
  • Federal Rules of Evidence — Rule 702: Testimony by Expert Witnesses (Cornell LII) ↗
  • Federal Rules of Evidence — Rule 1006: Summaries to Prove Content (Cornell LII) ↗
Core Principle

Never Overstate Illustrative Geometry

Always have technical reconstruction conclusions reviewed by appropriately qualified engineering, safety, or medical professionals.
